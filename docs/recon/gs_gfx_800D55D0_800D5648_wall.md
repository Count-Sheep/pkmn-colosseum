report. The `main/game/gs_gfx_candidate_800D55D0` object is incomplete and

89.666664% by changing branches. Both trials were reverted. The source is
unchanged by this attempt; no assembly or compiler-control workaround was
adopted. Further work needs a source-level shape or compiler-profile insight
